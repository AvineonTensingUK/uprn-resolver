One row per data element. Status as of 2026-10-23. "Unverified" means the publisher page was not read or did not settle
the point. Sources are those already cited in [RESEARCH.md](../RESEARCH.md), [DATASOURCES.md](../DATASOURCES.md),
[publish/LICENCE.md](../publish/LICENCE.md) and [candidate-sources.md](candidate-sources.md). Not legal advice.

Decision values: **published** (in the OGL release), **separate-table** (published or publishable only as its own
file under its own licence), **local-only** (never leaves the machine), **held** (built but not released pending an answer).
